## Anomalies & Responses

### growth-anomaly (severity: mid)
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: batch import/farming causing sudden spike, not natural development.
- **Impact**: scale stats distorted, totals and author counts inflated; obscures real activity and quality trends.
- **Recommendation**: official filter batch accounts and publish dedup metrics; dashboard add “excl. anomalies” new count.

### owner-cluster (severity: mid)
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: account uckkk using templates to mass-generate plugins without real function.
- **Impact**: inflates total count, lowers avg score; users overwhelmed by low-quality content in search and rankings.
- **Recommendation**: mark uckkk as batch account and downrank; trigger manual review or removal.

### dominant-owner (severity: low)
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: single account massive registration, same source as owner-cluster but focusing on share impact.
- **Impact**: uckkk occupies 11% of ecosystem, distorts author and activity rankings, breaks fairness.
- **Recommendation**: set automated author share alert; single account exceeding threshold (e.g., 100 plugins) enters review queue.

### npm-stale (severity: low)
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: release pipeline not synced with repo, or authors not npm publish timely.
- **Impact**: users get outdated builds, may miss features or contain bugs; drift breaks dependency resolution.
- **Recommendation**: official add warning in market for repo/npm mismatch; authors configure auto-publish.

### breaking-release (severity: high)
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: official introducing API changes in alpha stage; dsh-v0.1.6-alpha.2 and alpha.1 both breaking.
- **Impact**: plugins depending on old interfaces may crash; especially those with manifest.no-client-export.
- **Recommendation**: official publish migration guide; authors check client export; users backup configs before upgrade.
