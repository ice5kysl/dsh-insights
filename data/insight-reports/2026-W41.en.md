## Anomalies and Responses

### owner-cluster · mid · uckkk batch account
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: Template copying, automated script batch generation, pursuing quantity over quality.
- **Impact**: 1,286 plugins with avg 56 pollute leaderboards, search, category stats, hiding true ecosystem quality.
- **Recommendation**: DSH official should rate-limit uckkk, hide its plugins pending review, and label statistics as “excluding batch accounts”.

### owner-cluster · mid · CAI-MH batch account
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: Same template or low-quality duplication; 9 plugins avg 57, 1 without README.
- **Impact**: Small scale, but if uncontrolled may become another batch account, polluting specific categories.
- **Recommendation**: Official review CAI-MH’s 9 plugins; require README completion or downgrade/removal.

### dominant-owner · low · uckkk scale monopoly
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: Batch account exploits loose registration review, rapidly scales to form long-tail head.
- **Impact**: uckkk alone holds 9% of plugins with avg 56, distorting author rankings and plugin counts; users may be flooded with low-quality content.
- **Recommendation**: Separate “batch/suspected template” authors in public lists, and lower such plugins’ weight in default search results.

### npm-stale · low · version drift
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: Authors did not sync npm after release, or npm package was squatted/mismatched causing latest to lag repo.
- **Impact**: Users install outdated versions via npm, missing fixes/features, possibly incompatible with current DSH.
- **Recommendation**: Official provide npm sync detection tool, show “version behind” warning on detail page, and push authors to one-click republish.

### breaking-release · high · dsh-v0.2.1-alpha.2
- **Possible cause (hypothesis)**: Official intentionally introduced breaking changes to fix architecture/security, normal iteration but high risk.
- **Impact**: All plugins relying on old APIs may fail after upgrade, especially those unpublished or unmaintained.
- **Recommendation**: Plugin authors immediately check changelog and update plugins; official publish migration guide and grace period; users should delay upgrading to alpha.2 until stable.
